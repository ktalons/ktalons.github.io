## Who I am

I'm a cybersecurity engineer based in **Tucson, Arizona**. Dean's List, Fall 2024 and Spring 2025.

## What I do

For the past year and a half I've worked as a **Student OT SOC Analyst Engineer** for the University of Arizona's Facilities Management department — monitoring building automation systems (HVAC, metering, electricity management) across three campus ICS sites that keep the lights on during peak Tucson Electric Power demand. I built out a custom **ELK-based SIEM**, deployed **Filebeat → Logstash** ingestion pipelines for **Suricata**, **Zeek**, and custom ICS alerts, and ran incident response on **Modbus**, **BACnet**, and **ERSPAN** traffic. It's been a real-world exposure to OT/ICS security that few new grads get.

Alongside that, I serve as **CTF Captain** of [The Cyber Saguaros Club](https://github.com/Saguaros-CyberHub). I lead competitive teams of 4–8 across national competitions, including taking our team to **100th out of 4,214 teams** in NCL Fall 2025 and qualifying for the **WWRCDC 2026** Western Regional invitational.
