## Who I am

I'm a cybersecurity analyst and engineer based in **Tucson, Arizona**. Dean's List: Fall 2024, Spring 2025, Fall 2025, and Spring 2026.

## What I did

For a year and a half I worked as a **Student OT SOC Analyst Engineer** for the University of Arizona's Facilities Management department, monitoring building automation systems (HVAC, metering, electricity management) across three campus ICS sites that keep the lights on during peak Tucson Electric Power demand. I built out a custom **ELK-based SIEM**, deployed **Filebeat → Logstash** ingestion pipelines for **Suricata**, **Zeek**, and custom ICS alerts, and ran incident response on **Modbus**, **BACnet**, and **ERSPAN** traffic. The role gave me production OT/ICS security experience few new grads have.

Alongside that, I served as **CTF Captain** of [The Cyber Saguaros Club](https://cybersaguaros.com/). I led competitive teams of 4 to 8 across national competitions, taking our team to **100th out of 4,214** in NCL Fall 2025 and qualifying for the **WWRCDC 2026** Western Regional invitational.

## How I work

I'm building [**TalonSocLab**](/projects/talonsoclab/) in public: a home SOC on hardware I own, running Wazuh, Suricata, and Sysmon in docker-compose, with a detection-engineering rule pack proven against Atomic Red Team, a vulnerable AD attack/defense lab, and a honeynet feeding an IOC pipeline. I keep it deliberately deterministic so it can feed [**CASA**](https://github.com/ktalons/casa-ai-agent), my agentic SOC-analysis layer and senior capstone, which does the reasoning on top. I learn by building the thing, and it is all on GitHub.
