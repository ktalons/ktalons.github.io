**Repo:** [github.com/ktalons/cybersec-discord-bot](https://github.com/ktalons/cybersec-discord-bot)

Built for The Cyber Saguaros Club community:

- **Email verification** — gates server access to verified club members
- **Roster management** — automated role assignment based on team membership
- **Giveaway system** — fair RNG-based prize draws with audit log
- **Google Calendar integration** — syncs club events to a public calendar
- **CTFtime announcements** — auto-posts upcoming CTFs to a dedicated channel

Python, async-first, deployed via Docker. Solves real day-to-day club-ops friction.
