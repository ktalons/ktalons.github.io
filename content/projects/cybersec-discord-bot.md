**Repo:** [github.com/ktalons/cybersec-discord-bot](https://github.com/ktalons/cybersec-discord-bot)

A modern Discord bot for cybersecurity clubs and communities. Python, async-first, deployed via Docker. Solved real day-to-day club-ops friction.

## Features

- **Email verification:** gated server access to verified club members.
- **Roster management:** automated role assignment based on team membership.
- **Giveaway system:** ran fair RNG-based prize draws with an audit log.
- **Google Calendar integration:** synced club events to a public calendar.
- **CTFtime announcements:** auto-posted upcoming CTFs to a dedicated channel.

## Battle-tested at Cyber Saguaros

Built and operated for the [Cyber Saguaros Club](https://cybersaguaros.com/) at the University of Arizona during my time as CTF Captain. Ran in production handling member verification, weekly CTF announcements, and team roster updates. The codebase remains public for anyone running a similar club.
